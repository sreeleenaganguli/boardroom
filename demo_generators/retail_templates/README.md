# Customer Sales & Retail templates

This demo-only package includes six templates, each with exactly 15 columns, data types, sample values, business rules, relationships, suggested KPIs, visualizations, and questions in `template_catalog.json`.

```powershell
py -3 demo_generators\retail_templates\generate_retail_datasets.py --rows 10000 --out-dir demo_data\retail_templates
```

Templates: Customer Sales Transactions, Retail Store Performance, E-commerce Orders, Product Sales Performance, Customer Purchase Behaviour, and Regional Sales Performance.
